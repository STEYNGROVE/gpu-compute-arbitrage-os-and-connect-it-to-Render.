# Customer acquisition readiness

Existing public enquiry form/service information at `/request-quote`, bounded `/api/public/quote-info` and `/api/public/quote-request`, owner sales workspace, customer intelligence, publication-aware feed discovery and hunter review are preserved. They target inference, training/fine-tuning, rendering and batch processing. Transcription and research fit recorded workload descriptions; no demand or customer interest is inferred merely from those categories. Closed owner-managed customer provisioning remains; no uncontrolled registration was enabled.

The migration repair makes enquiry, lead, hunter and documentary-evidence storage reproducible on a fresh or compatible upgraded database. Database-backed public enquiries fail closed when authoritative storage is unavailable. No in-memory production durability is claimed. Anonymous/invalid/customer access tests cover administration while the intended public enquiry paths retain validation and rate limits.

For source-backed leads, preserve source URL, retrieval/publication time where genuinely supplied, source digest, title/organization/workload/budget/deadline/geography/eligibility/contact route where actually published, and qualification status. Absence is not zero budget, eligibility or funding. Manual source/context metadata remains UNVERIFIED. Existing source/organization uniqueness and source evidence rules prevent duplicate intake; automatic outreach/bids are unavailable. SAM.gov requires an authorized API key and current, explicit-timezone response deadline; expired, absent, malformed or timezone-ambiguous deadlines are skipped independently so one notice cannot poison a valid batch.

Fresh public evidence in this audit contains 48 Akash GPU order observations and public supplier rows, not 48 paying customers. No enterprise RFQ with a verified budget or organization-specific acceptance was collected through a credentialed SAM call. Source chain freshness, procurement authority, order award and funding remain unverified. Feed registry authorization and supplier/RFQ credentials are external dependencies for broader collection.

The minimum safe sales workflow is owner review of a real enquiry or permitted procurement notice, verification of identity/contact and full scope, nonbinding all-cost estimate, explicit contract/subcontracting and data-handling review, followed by separate verified-order gates. No unsolicited messages, binding offer, signature, purchase or revenue posting was sent by this task. No actual paying customer has been independently established.
