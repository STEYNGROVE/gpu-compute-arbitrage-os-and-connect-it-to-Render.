# Commercial quotation verification

The existing indicative planner remains available with contract-required, availability-unverified and purchasing-disabled flags. Pre-funded plan assessment separately binds committed customer price, maximum contractual price, duration, GPU count/model/VRAM, bandwidth, region, uptime and workload reference to six signed receipt categories. The signed complete-cost plan includes rental, setup, commission, transaction, conversion, slippage, network, storage, transfer, contingency and applicable taxes in one supported minor-unit currency. A maximum procurement budget is not committed revenue.

Estimated net = committed customer revenue minus every listed cost. Configurable positive net and margin policies reject insufficient outcomes. Non-USD catalog comparison refuses to invent FX. Unknown supplier costs or eligibility cannot support a binding quote; plan verification requires independent all-cost, tax/FX and firm supplier evidence. Defaults install no trusted collectors, so manual claims cannot pass. Historical settled/paid stages do not reserve funds for a new job.

Repairs select newest valid observations before the 500-record limit, preserve deterministic input order for equal times, rank screening-passing results before cheaper hardware mismatches and flag stale/future/invalid prices. More-than-500 and ranking regressions are tested. Dashboard mount coalescing, abort/unmount and expired-auth behavior do not retry mutations or turn a review into procurement. Proposal parsing failures appear beside the form and clear on edit/retry.

Public catalog responses and passing mathematical fixtures do not establish a currently firm, profitable customer quotation. No binding quote was issued. No supplier fees, tax exemption, availability SLA or currency peg was independently established in production. All such external assumptions remain review blockers for Mode B; Mode A only exposes explicitly indicative pricing and enquiries.
